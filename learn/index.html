Four ideas that make the rest easier.

01

It predicts, it doesn't know

AI tools guess the most likely next word. That's why they can be brilliant and confidently wrong.

02

You're the editor

Treat what it gives you as a first draft from a fast, eager intern. You still decide.

03

Talk to it like a person

Context, examples, and "here's what I'm really trying to do" beat clever tricks.

04

Small and often wins

Ten minutes a day beats a weekend binge. Curiosity compounds.
