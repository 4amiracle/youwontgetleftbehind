A very short history of humans making things

Every leap felt like being
left behind to somebody.

And every one was learned the same way, person by person, by someone showing someone else.

  1. ~1.7M yrs ago◭

    The hand axe

    The first tool. Someone figured it out, then showed someone else.

  2. ~3500 BCE◎

    The wheel

    Took centuries to catch on. Nobody was in a rush.

  3. 1440▤

    The printing press

    Knowledge stopped belonging to the few.

  4. 1843∿

    Ada Lovelace

    Wrote the first algorithm for a machine that didn't exist yet.

  5. 1945▦

    ENIAC

    Programmed largely by six women who learned it from wiring diagrams.

  6. 1969⇄

    The first network message

    "LO." The system crashed before "LOGIN." A beautiful start.

  7. 1984☺

    The computer says hello

    Personal computers got friendly enough to sit on a kitchen table.

  8. 1991◍

    The World Wide Web

    Anyone could publish. Everyone had to learn what a "link" was.

  9. 2007▯

    The smartphone

    The internet moved into our pockets, and our hands.

  10. 2022✦

    AI you can talk to

    Suddenly the machine speaks our language. Here we go again.

  11. now●

    You

    Right on time. Let's figure out the next part together.
