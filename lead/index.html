What I'm learning

Principles for leading people through it.

01

Name the fear out loud

"Will this replace me?" is the real question in the room. Answer it honestly before anything else.

02

Make it safe to be bad at it

Nobody learns while they're trying to look like they already know.

03

Start with one tiny habit

Behavior changes through small, repeated wins, not mandates or launch emails.

04

Learn alongside, not ahead

Leaders who show their own learning give everyone else permission to try.
