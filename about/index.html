Why this exists

Nobody should have to figure this out alone.

Every big leap in technology, from the printing press to the personal computer to the phone in your pocket, left some people feeling like they'd missed the train. AI is that moment for a lot of us.

I'm not an engineer or an AI researcher. I'm a curious person who decided to stop feeling behind and start learning in public: the questions, the wins, the embarrassing mistakes. If I can do it, you can too.

This is a place to learn together, talk honestly about how change feels, and build real things side by side. And then to log off, go outside, and remember we're humans on a pretty incredible planet.
