If you've been nodding along in conversations about AI while quietly wondering what everyone is talking about, this is for you. You're not behind. You're right on time.

## What it actually is

The AI tools most people use today, the ones you can type to and talk with, are trained on enormous amounts of writing. Over time they get very good at one thing: predicting what words should come next.

That sounds simple. It turns out that when you get *really* good at predicting the next word, you can draft emails, explain ideas, brainstorm, summarize, and even write code.

> It doesn't know things the way you do. It predicts. That's why it can be brilliant and confidently wrong in the same sentence.

## What it's good at

- Getting you unstuck from a blank page
- Explaining something again, differently, as many times as you need
- Turning messy notes into something organized
- Helping you build small tools, even if you've never coded

## The one mindset that helps

Think of it as a fast, eager collaborator who has read a lot but has never met you. Give it context. Tell it what you're really trying to do. Then **you** stay the editor.

## Try this today

Open any AI chat tool and type: *"I'm completely new to AI. Ask me three questions about my life or work, then suggest one small way you could help me this week."*

That's it. That's the first step.
